# Waypoints and banners

## Waypoints

A waypoint is a place of your own, with a name and a color. The minimap and the map of the world show your waypoints. A waypoint out of the minimap stays on its side.

- Press **B** to add a waypoint at your position.
- On the map of the world, right click an empty place to add a waypoint.
- On the map of the world, right click a waypoint to change or remove it.

You can have 256 waypoints. A name has 32 characters at most.

### Deaths

A death adds a waypoint at the place of the death. The last 3 deaths stay.

## Banners

Use a filled map or a [survey map](survey-maps.md) on a banner. The banner then shows on your minimap and on your map of the world, with its color and its name.

- Use a map on the banner again to remove the marker.
- Move the mouse above the marker to see the name of the banner.
- A broken banner goes away from your maps. The marker follows a new color or a new name.
- Only you see the banners that you marked.

The setting **Views of the banners** selects the minimap, the map of the world, or both. The button **Layers** of the map of the world hides the markers.

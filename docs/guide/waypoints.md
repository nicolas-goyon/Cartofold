# Waypoints and banners

## Waypoints

A waypoint is a place of your own, with a name and a color. The minimap, the map of the world and the [markers in the world](#markers-in-the-world) show your waypoints. A waypoint out of the minimap stays on its side.

- Press **B** to add a waypoint at your position.
- On the map of the world, right click an empty place to add a waypoint.
- On the map of the world, right click a waypoint to change or remove it.

You can have 256 waypoints. A name has 32 characters at most.

### Deaths

A death adds a waypoint at the place of the death. The last 3 deaths stay.

## Banners

Use a filled map or a [survey map](survey-maps.md) on a banner. The banner then shows on your minimap and on your map of the world, with its color and its name.

- Use a map on the banner again to remove the marker.
- Move the mouse above the marker to see the name of the banner.
- A broken banner goes away from your maps. The marker follows a new color or a new name.
- Only you see the banners that you marked.

The setting **Views of the banners** selects the minimap, the map of the world, or both. The button **Layers** of the map of the world hides the markers. The banners also show in the world: the button **HUD** of the layer **Banners** hides them there.

## Markers in the world

The screen of the game shows your waypoints, your banners and the markers of the layers at their places in the world. Each marker shows its icon, its name and its distance in blocks.

- Press **U** to show or hide the markers in the world.
- A near marker is above a far marker.
- By default, the markers nearer than 50 blocks and farther than 1000 blocks do not show.
- A marker shows 1 block above its position. The setting `height` changes this height.
- The button **Layers** of the map of the world has a button **HUD** for each layer. It shows or hides the markers of the layer in the world. A layer of a mod can stay out of the world: its button **HUD** is then off.

The [settings](settings.md#markers-in-the-world) also change the opacity, the size, and the names and the distances.
